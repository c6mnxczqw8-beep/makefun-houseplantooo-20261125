# Houseplant OOO candidates (draft pass)

One auto-reply each, at most 120 characters: what a
houseplant's out-of-office message would say.

1. Before offices: "Out of office: I am a plant. I was out
   of office before offices. Water me Thursday." -- grand
   and practical; the winner adds the refusal to be hurried
   by human schedules.
2. Window: "Away: photosynthesising until spring. For urgent
   matters, talk to the window. I will not be watered by
   your timeline." -- the timeline line is very funny, near
   the limit.
3. Slow reply: "Thanks for your email. I grow 2cm a year.
   Expect a reply at that pace." -- elegant.
4. Sun leave: "On leave until the sun moves. It moves daily.
   I remain on leave." -- a logical trap, plant-perfect.
5. Emergency contact: "If this is urgent, my emergency
   contact is the rain." -- short and lovely.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1 has the grandeur, but
candidate 2 has the plant's complete management philosophy:
absence until spring, escalation path via the window, and a
flat refusal to be scheduled -- "I will not be watered by
your timeline" is the most plant sentence ever written
about project management.
